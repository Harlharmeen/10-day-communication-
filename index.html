The important part

Communication isn't a personality trait. It's a skill you can train.

What changes in 10 days

You won't become a perfect communicator. You'll become a more deliberate one.

Ten days won't magically transform you into a professional speaker. What it can give you is deliberate practice, practical tools and a better understanding of how you communicate.

10

Days of deliberate practice

Learn the principle. Apply it. Get feedback. Try again.

01

Say what you mean

Make your thoughts easier to understand.

Learn to express your ideas with less confusion, unnecessary wording and ambiguity — so people can follow what you're actually trying to say.

02

Speak with composure

Stop letting self-doubt control the conversation.

Practise speaking with greater composure and presence, even when you're being watched, evaluated or put on the spot.

03

Give your ideas direction

Know where your message is going.

Learn practical ways to organise your thoughts before and while speaking, so your message has a clear beginning, middle and point.

04

Make ideas memorable

Turn information into something people remember.

Learn how stories, examples and experiences can make your message easier to understand and more engaging.

05

Communicate on camera

Become more natural when the camera is on.

Practise speaking naturally and intentionally through video without trying to sound like someone you're not.

06

Hold attention

Present ideas with greater control.

Practise turning your ideas into a message an audience can follow, understand and remember.

07

Connect better

Become a more intentional conversationalist.

Learn to listen, contribute and respond in ways that make conversations more meaningful.

08

Handle difficult moments

Say difficult things without losing yourself.

Approach uncomfortable conversations with more clarity, empathy and intention.

The goal isn't to sound impressive. The goal is to communicate effectively.
